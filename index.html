Selected AI Systems & Research Projects

Local LLM Inference & Agentic Deployment — Qwen / SGLang / LiteLLM
Deployed a local Qwen model behind SGLang and an OpenAI-compatible LiteLLM interface over TLS, connected to agentic coding/tool workflows. Integrated DFlash2 speculative drafting and MTP, benchmarking a ~2.7× throughput gain (~93 → ~250 tokens/s) on the same local inference stack.
Qwen3.5-9B Multimodal Dietary Fine-Tuning — QLoRA
Built multimodal instruction data pairing egocentric meal images with structured food, portion, calorie, and item-count supervision with JSON-formatted outputs. Fine-tuned Qwen3.5-9B with QLoRA (rank r=16, quantized base weights), reducing mean calorie absolute error from 43.1% to 22.4% (48% relative reduction) and item-count MAE from 1.20 to 0.40 versus the untuned baseline.
Megatron Full-Parameter Fine-Tuning Study — Small-Data VLM Adaptation
Ran a Megatron-based full-parameter training experiment on the same dietary multimodal task and compared it against the parameter-efficient QLoRA approach. Diagnosed poor generalization in the small-data regime — despite training convergence, held-out calorie error worsened versus the untuned baseline.
DietAI24 — Retrieval-Augmented Food Identification & Calorie Estimation
Built sequence RAG for food identification and FNDDS-based calorie estimation using CLIP embeddings, vector retrieval, reranking, and SSIM gating to skip redundant frames.
Neo-EEGPT — EEG Seizure Detection & Android Deployment
Trained an EEG Transformer for neonatal seizure detection with subject-wise validation; deployed to Android with INT8 PTQ, reducing model size by 3.53×.
